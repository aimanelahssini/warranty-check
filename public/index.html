Warranty Check

See if a laptop is still under warranty, in a second.

Or check another device:

Pick a brand, or just paste the serial and press Enter.

On the laptop's sticker

Underneath the laptop, labelled S/N, Serial or Service Tag (Dell). Lenovo serials are 8 characters, Dell service tags 7.

Windows: paste this in PowerShell

(Get-CimInstance Win32_BIOS).SerialNumber

Linux: paste this in a terminal

sudo cat /sys/class/dmi/id/product_serial

Mac

Apple menu → About This Mac. The serial is on the first screen.

Lenovo results come straight from Lenovo. HP, Dell and Apple hide their warranty dates behind a captcha, so you finish on their site.
